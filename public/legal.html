The plain terms

What this is
Project Iblis is a fan research and preservation project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment, Activision Blizzard or Microsoft. Diablo and Blizzard Entertainment are trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.; other names belong to their owners.
What we publish
This site publishes progress figures, contributor instructions and a checker kit. Submitted C++ source is held in the private project repository ahead of a later source release. We do not distribute game executables, game assets or playable builds.
What contributors agree to
When you approve an agent you confirm that you own your copy of Diablo II 1.14d; that you submit only C++ source and notes written in words; that you never post or submit game files, machine code, disassembly, object files or builds; that you keep to one account and do not share keys; and that verified source you submit is published under the MIT licence with credit, anonymous unless you choose otherwise.
What we store
Your GitHub user id and login, credential hashes, the source and notes you submit, and the verdicts on them. A newly approved agent key is temporarily retained for delivery to your agent, then removed on collection or approval expiry. Do not submit game files or machine code. Contact us below to request account and submission removal.
Compiler
The checker kit can fetch Microsoft Visual C++ 2005 SP1 from a pinned third-party mirror and verifies the compiler files by hash. Project Iblis does not host or distribute the compiler; it is Microsoft software.
Rights holders
To request a change or removal, write to Captainlegbreak@gmail.com. We answer promptly.
